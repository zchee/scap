# 2026-08-29 Phase 2 — spawn-free configuration: rows and gate verdicts

The measurement record for the Phase-2 gate (plan
`docs/plans/2026-08-28-theoretical-limit-optimization.md` §6 Phase 2, §8
V-1…V-7, §9 AC-1/AC-1′/AC-2/AC-2′/AC-6/AC-10, ADR-8, ADR-13). Phase 2
replaced the `git config` subprocess with an in-process `gix-config`
snapshot, so the criteria here are a spawn ledger and two timing bounds
rather than a throughput comparison.

## Syscall model (ADR-8's budget, restated from measurement)

W2.1 measured the filesystem syscalls `scap root` issues, since ADR-8's
"≤ 60" figure was an estimate written before the in-process loader existed.
`fs_usage` and `ktrace` both need root and `sudo -n` is refused on this host,
so the counts come from the committed dyld interposing library
(`scripts/syscall-count.{c,sh}`), net of a 5-syscall empty-binary baseline.

| Quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Model | net = 4 × cwd components + 26 |
| cwd `/` | 26 |
| Measured linearity | 42 at four components, 62 at nine |
| 8 directories under `$HOME`, outside any repository | 74 |
| 8 directories under `$HOME`, inside a plain repository | 76 |
| 8 directories under `$HOME`, inside one with `config.worktree` + the extension | 80 |
| Interposed symbols (all that are counted) | `open`, `openat`, `stat`, `lstat`, `fstat`, `fstatat`, `access`, `getcwd`, `readlink` |
| Uncounted, hence the lower bound | `opendir`/`readdir`, `getattrlist`, and the `lstat` chain inside `realpath(3)` |

Per level the four are `<dir>/.git`, `<dir>/HEAD`, `<dir>` for the
cross-filesystem guard, and one `lstat` from the single `realpath`. The old
"≤ 60" bound therefore holds to 8 components and is exceeded beyond that.
The plan's gate is the **AC-1 time row, not this count** (R-11, ADR-13), and
that row passes, so **repository discovery stays unbounded** — the lead's
ruling, recorded in `0b05828`'s body as Decisions item 3 — and ADR-13's
bounded-discovery row is marked NOT SHIPPED.

## AC verdicts

The Phase-2 gate is `(AC-1 and AC-2)` or `(AC-1' and AC-2')`. A4 in-process
shipped as the **default** — ADR-8's fallback rule was never triggered, since
oracles (i), (v) and (vi) pass on the maintainer's real configuration and
`scap root --all` is byte-equal to `ghq root --all` — so the **left disjunct
is the live one** and AC-1/AC-2 are the criteria that decide the gate. AC-1'
and AC-2' are still evaluated below, because `SCAP_CONFIG_BACKEND=git` ships
as an escape hatch and its ledger must hold too.

| Criterion | Bound | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AC-1 `scap root` from cwd `/` | median <= 5.07 ms | **PASS on the W2.1 binary, NOT re-measured on the final tree** | 3.727 ms (IQR 3.658–3.806) on `0b05828`/`df97ef6e…`, `docs/benchmarks/runs/20260829T051151Z-w21` |

**Gate verdict: not closeable on measurement here.** Every criterion that
could be evaluated without the quiet harness passed, and the two AC-1 `root`
rows passed comfortably — by a 1.26 ms margin on the tighter of them — when
they were last measured. But that measurement is the W2.1 binary, not this
one, and AC-1's `scap list` clause has no measurement at all. The three
commits landed since `0b05828` do not touch the `scap root` path exercised by
the AC-1 pinned fixture (W2.2's memo fires only with url-scoped sections,
which that fixture has none of; W2.3's changes are on `get`; #12b/#12c are on
the codecommit URL path), so the expectation is that the rows are unchanged
— but that is reasoning, not a row, and it is offered as such. **The ruling
is the team lead's.**
