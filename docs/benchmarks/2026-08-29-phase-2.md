# 2026-08-29 Phase 2 — spawn-free configuration: rows and gate verdicts

The measurement record for the Phase-2 gate (plan
`docs/plans/2026-08-28-theoretical-limit-optimization.md` §6 Phase 2, §8
V-1…V-7, §9 AC-1/AC-1′/AC-2/AC-2′/AC-6/AC-10, ADR-8, ADR-13). Phase 2
replaced the `git config` subprocess with an in-process `gix-config`
snapshot, so the criteria here are a spawn ledger and two timing bounds
rather than a throughput comparison.

## Syscall model (ADR-8's budget, restated from measurement)

W2.1 measured the filesystem syscalls `scap root` issues, since ADR-8's
"≤ 60" figure was an estimate written before the in-process loader existed.
`fs_usage` and `ktrace` both need root and `sudo -n` is refused on this host,
so the counts come from the committed dyld interposing library
(`scripts/syscall-count.{c,sh}`), net of a 5-syscall empty-binary baseline.

| Quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Model | net = 4 × cwd components + 26 |
| cwd `/` | 26 |
| Measured linearity | 42 at four components, 62 at nine |
| 8 directories under `$HOME`, outside any repository | 74 |
| 8 directories under `$HOME`, inside a plain repository | 76 |
| 8 directories under `$HOME`, inside one with `config.worktree` + the extension | 80 |
| Interposed symbols (all that are counted) | `open`, `openat`, `stat`, `lstat`, `fstat`, `fstatat`, `access`, `getcwd`, `readlink` |
| Uncounted, hence the lower bound | `opendir`/`readdir`, `getattrlist`, and the `lstat` chain inside `realpath(3)` |

Per level the four are `<dir>/.git`, `<dir>/HEAD`, `<dir>` for the
cross-filesystem guard, and one `lstat` from the single `realpath`. The old
"≤ 60" bound therefore holds to 8 components and is exceeded beyond that.
The plan's gate is the **AC-1 time row, not this count** (R-11, ADR-13), and
that row passes, so **repository discovery stays unbounded** — the lead's
ruling, recorded in `0b05828`'s body as Decisions item 3 — and ADR-13's
bounded-discovery row is marked NOT SHIPPED.

### AC-1's `root` rows in both forms

Because the run's floor drifted from the frozen one, AC-1 is evaluated two
ways and both are recorded: **(a)** the frozen absolute form the plan states,
median <= 5.07 ms; and **(b)** the same-run paired form matching the
criterion's stated intent — `scap root` without `SCAP_ROOT` within 0.5 ms of
`scap root` with it — which is immune to machine-level drift between runs, as
AC-6's own restatement is. Bootstrap CIs are the committed
`scripts/bench-compare.py::bootstrap_median_diff_ci`, seed 0, n = 10,000.

| Row | Median | (a) vs 5.07 ms | (b) paired vs 0.5 ms | Paired 95 % CI | Forms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `root_pinned_root_cwd` (gated) | 4.436 ms | **PASS** | **PASS** (+0.176) | [-0.049, 0.680] | agree |
| `root_pinned_inside8_cwd` (gated) | 4.229 ms | **PASS** | **PASS** (-0.032) | [-0.181, 0.041] | agree |
| `root_pinned_outside8_cwd` (reported) | 4.043 ms | **PASS** | **PASS** (-0.218) | [-0.332, -0.108] | agree |

Both forms agree on every row, so nothing here turns on which is primary.

**Caveat on the cwd-`/` row.** `root_pinned_root_cwd` is markedly noisier
than its neighbours — sigma 0.57 ms against 0.17–0.23 — and its IQR's upper
end, 5.087 ms, sits just *above* the 5.07 ms absolute bound. Its median
passes with 0.63 ms of headroom, and its paired CI extends past the 0.5 ms
paired bound to 0.680. So the row passes on both medians while neither
form's margin is comfortable, and it is the one row where a re-measure could
plausibly land differently. The other two are unambiguous. A 60-run
tightening re-measure was authorised as supporting evidence but not run: it
was queued behind the three deferred groups, whose window never reopened.

**Gate verdict: AC-1's `root` clause and AC-2 are met on the final tree; the
gate is not fully closeable here.** What remains unmeasured is AC-1's `scap
list` CPU-difference clause, which has no measurement on any binary, and
AC-6's guard on the Phase-2 binary. **The ruling is the team lead's.**
